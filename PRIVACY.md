# Privacy Policy for PocketTally

Last updated: 17 October 2026

PocketTally is an income and expense tracker made by Lumio Apps. It works
offline; the only time it goes online is when you tap "Check for updates"
(see Permissions). **We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.**
We run no server, and the app has no account, no ads and no analytics or
tracking tools.

## What stays on your phone

Everything you enter is stored only on your device: entries, categories,
budgets, Lend & Borrow records, savings goals, your name and settings, and the
bill photos and profile photo you choose to add. The PIN of the app lock is
stored only as a salted hash. None of this is sent anywhere.

## Backups and exports

Backup files, CSV files and PDF reports are created only when you ask for
them, or by the automatic backup you can switch on in Settings. They are saved
on your device, in the place you choose (or in the Documents/PocketTally
folder for automatic backups). The app never uploads them. If you move a file
to another service, that service's own privacy policy applies.

## Permissions

- **Notifications:** to show the reminders you set. They are planned and shown
  on your phone.
- **Start after restart:** to plan your reminders again after the phone
  restarts.
- **Photos and camera:** the app opens the system photo picker or camera only
  when you add a bill photo or a profile photo, and receives only the picture
  you choose.
- **Internet and install apps:** used only for **Settings › Check for
  updates**, and only when you tap it. The app then asks GitHub
  (api.github.com) for the newest PocketTally release, and if you choose
  "Update" it downloads the APK from GitHub and opens the Android installer.
  GitHub receives the usual technical data of any web request (such as your IP
  address), as with any website you open. The app sends none of your data
  and checks nothing in the background.

## Children

PocketTally is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your phone. Backup files you
saved yourself stay where you saved them, and you can delete them at any time.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.
